# Demo media

TikDog's demo uses real videos downloaded from Pexels under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/), reviewed on October 18, 2026. The license permits free use and modification in apps, including commercial apps. It prohibits reselling unaltered footage, redistribution as a stock-media service, and implying endorsement.

The four dog identities, owners, captions, engagement numbers, and activity are fictional demo data. The stock creators and dogs do not endorse TikDog. Videos were trimmed to at most 10 seconds, resized/cropped to 540 × 960, encoded as H.264, and muted. Thumbnails and avatars are still frames from the same clips. All four clips and their thumbnails are bundled for offline playback; no media account or network access is needed.
