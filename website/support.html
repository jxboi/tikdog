Report unsafe content

Open the options on the video, comment, or dog profile and choose Report. You can also block an account from those options. Reports hide content for you immediately. We aim to review reports within 24 hours. For urgent animal-safety concerns, also contact the appropriate local animal welfare service.

Manage your account

Sign in with Facebook or Apple. TikDog has no separate email/password account or password-reset flow. Recover access through your chosen provider. Open Profile > Settings to manage blocked accounts, read policies, sign out, or delete your TikDog account. Deleting TikDog does not delete your Facebook or Apple account.
