Information we collect

We receive your name, email address and provider account identifier when you choose Facebook or Apple sign-in. Facebook may also provide your profile picture. Sign in with Apple may provide a private relay address. Supabase manages your TikDog identity and session; TikDog does not ask for or store your Facebook or Apple password. We collect the dog profiles, photos, videos (including recorded sound), captions, hashtags, and comments you choose to submit. We also store likes, follows, bookmarks, votes, content reports, terms acceptance, and account status to run the community. Avoid sharing personal or sensitive information in public content.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate accounts, display approved content, save your choices, rank videos using engagement and recency, prevent abuse, moderate contributions, and respond to requests. Camera and microphone access is used when you record; the system photo picker provides only items you select. We do not request contacts or location. We do not sell personal information, use advertising SDKs, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Visibility and sharing

Approved dog profiles, videos, comments, and aggregate engagement counts are visible to everyone, including guests. Your bookmarks, email address, content reports, and blocked-account list are private. Pending content is available to its owner and authorized moderators. Media uses private storage with access links that expire after 15 minutes; people can still save or share copies they can access. Blocking hides accounts within TikDog; it cannot recall saved copies or immediately invalidate existing access links.

Service providers

Supabase hosts authentication, application data, and uploaded media on our behalf. Hosting infrastructure processes network information, such as IP addresses and technical logs, for delivery and security. Apple or Meta processes your chosen social sign-in. TikDog uses Facebook only for authentication, without the Facebook advertising or analytics SDK. These providers process data according to their service terms and privacy protections. We may disclose information to comply with applicable obligations or protect people and animals from harm. Data may be processed outside your country depending on the configured hosting region.

Retention and deletion

Account content and engagement remain until you remove them or delete your account, unless content is removed through moderation. Start account deletion in Profile > Settings > Delete account. Deletion first disables new contributions, revokes stored Apple authorization when applicable, removes uploaded media, and deletes the account and associated profiles, comments, reports, and engagement. If a service interruption prevents completion, the account remains disabled and deletion can be retried. Moderation audit entries contain the content identifier, action, reason, and moderator identifier, without retaining deleted media or comment text; we remove these entries after 90 days. Provider security logs and backup copies follow the hosting provider's retention settings; deleted material is not restored to active use. Copies saved by other people are outside our control.

Your choices

Browse as a guest, decline camera or microphone permission, choose which photos to share, remove your own videos, block accounts, sign out, or delete your account.
